# Source attribution

These examples adapt selected notebooks and scripts from [NVIDIA Kumo Engine](https://github.com/NVIDIA/kumo-engine), [NVIDIA Structured Data Models](https://github.com/NVIDIA/structured-data-models), Kumo documentation, earlier Kumo SDK examples, and this repository's existing notebooks.
The [source map](SOURCE_MAP.csv) records each selected source and its destination, including consolidated duplicates and the excluded obsolete hosted-project example.
The examples have been modified for the documented platform setup, published APIs, batch outputs and repository layout.

Copies of the upstream licenses are retained with their original filenames:

- [NVIDIA Kumo Engine license](LICENSES/nvidia-kumo-engine/LICENSE)
- [NVIDIA Structured Data Models license](LICENSES/nvidia-structured-data-models/LICENSE)

These license copies describe their respective upstream sources; they do not assign a new license to unrelated Kumo source material.
Source copyright and license notices are retained where present.
Public datasets are not bundled; their sources, terms and preparation requirements are described in the relevant examples.
